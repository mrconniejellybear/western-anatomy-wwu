This page contains every source used in the creation of westernanatomy.com, with articles grouped by publication and sorted alphabetically within. This page will see regular updates as the model is updated with new entries.

Kenhub was my primary source of information for most entries, alongside PubMed, Britannica, Radiopedia, Cleveland Clinic, three physical textbooks in my possession, and a handfull of brain dissection videos.

If not from the afformentioned, information came from a legitimate medical journal or a similarly credibile .org/.edu site. If any information or citations seem incorrect, please contact me using my links at the bottom, or by e-mailing me here.
